The numbers, for reference

Marches earned
5 × √km, where 1,350 steps ≈ 1 km
Claiming
Once a day, for that day’s steps from the Health app, up to 30,000; unclaimed days are lost; every claim is posted to the claim log
Disputes
Anyone else can dispute a claim for 24 hours; the other players then vote for up to 24 hours more; nothing is frozen meanwhile
Voiding
Needs a majority of the other players who have logged in within the last 24 hours; anyone who hasn’t doesn’t count. A voided claim’s marches come off the claimant’s next claim
Empty cell
1 march
Occupied cell
2 marches and a band of yours beside it. The band loses a soldier per wall course, per gun covering the cell and per enemy soldier guarding it, and needs one left over to walk in
Moving a band
1 march per 3 cells, over your own ground only
Guarding
A band guards its own cell and the six around it; it dies defending if the cell falls
River
Can’t be marched into. A bridge costs 1 march + 40 timber + 20 stone and is ready 24 hours later; then borders and supply cross it. Someone else’s bridge costs 2 marches. A band beside a bridge can break it for 2 marches; the river is then bare until a new bridge is built
The map
120 × 120 cells, wrapping at every edge
Wall
30 stone a course, three courses
Tower
40 stone + 30 timber; garrison 1, 2, then 4 by level; guns kill 2, 4, then 6 and reach 1, 2, then 3 cells; keeps every cell of yours in that reach supplied
Soldier
25 iron + 40 grain to muster at a castle, where he joins the band; he eats every tick thereafter
Harvest
Every 4 hours — 6 ticks a day, banking up to 3 days while you're away
Yields per tick
Forest 2 timber · Quarry 2 stone · Vein 1 iron · Field 3 grain
