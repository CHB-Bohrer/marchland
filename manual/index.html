The exact numbers
Steps to marches
Claim once a day, for that day only, up to 30,000 steps. 4,000 steps earn 9 marches, 10,000 earn 14, 20,000 earn 19, 30,000 earn 24. Days you don’t claim are lost.
Empty square
1 march, beside a square you already hold.
Enemy square
2 marches, win or lose, with a band of yours standing beside it. Your soldiers face the defenders one duel at a time: wall courses, guarding soldiers, castle guns, tower units and trebuchets. A scroll shows your chance before you commit, and how many soldiers pass 70%. Win and you carry off a share of the loser’s stores.
Harvest
Every 4 hours: forest 2 timber, quarry 2 stone, iron mine 1 iron, farm 3 grain. Only squares joined to one of your castles by your own ground pay. Up to 3 days bank while you are away.
Soldier
25 iron and 40 grain at a castle. Eats grain every 4 hours. Moving is free over your own ground: 3 hours a square, 1 hour a square along a live road. A band standing still guards its own square and the six around it.
River and bridge
Rivers can’t be marched into. A bridge costs 1 march, 40 timber and 20 stone and takes 24 hours. A band beside a bridge can break it for 2 marches.
Wall
30 stone a course, up to three. Each course is one more duel for an attacker.
Tower
40 stone and 30 timber. Health 10, 20 or 30 by level; eats 1, 2 or 3 grain every 4 hours; makes attacks within 1, 2 or 3 squares harder. Mends 1 health every 4 hours for 1 stone.
Trebuchet
240 timber, 160 iron and 120 stone, built at a castle; eats 2 grain every 4 hours. Fired by hand at an enemy wall, tower or castle up to 3 squares away: 1 stone a shot, 2 damage, once every 4 hours. It moves with your bands and almost always dies if caught alone.
Road
1 march, 20 timber and 10 stone, a day to build. Roads only change speed, and only while they join a standing castle.
Tearing down
Walls, towers, bridges and roads of your own, at once and for no marches: half the cost comes back. Castles can’t be torn down.
New castle
All six squares around the site must be yours (river squares count). It takes a day, needs a soldier in the field to garrison it, and you pay when it stands: 260 timber, 200 stone and 60 iron for the second, twice that for the third, and so on. Castles stand at least 7 squares apart, never on a farm.
Feeding a castle
A castle must be joined by your own ground to a farm you hold. Cut off, it loses health and falls after 5 days; rejoined, it heals at the same rate. A castle that falls, starved or battered down, stays fallen for a full day; after that a connected farm raises it again from 1 health. Whoever seizes a ruin gets 50 marches.
Out of the game
No castle standing and no soldiers anywhere: your name comes off the list and your squares are destroyed. You may start again with a fresh keep.
Staying away
After 3 days without opening the game you lose one bare square a day, outermost first. Anything built on a square keeps it safe.
Caravan
A gift of stone, timber, iron or grain, with or without a sealed letter of up to 280 characters. No marches. It takes 40 minutes a square between the two keeps (6 hours at least, 2 days at most) and costs you 1 grain every 4 hours on the road.
Gifting land
A bare square of yours that touches the other player’s ground. Free, instant, and it can’t be taken back.
Claims
Every claim is public. With a screenshot of your step count it stands. Without one, anyone may dispute it for 24 hours and the others vote for 24 more: more than half of at least 3 votes voids it, and the marches come off your next claim.
Joining
A newcomer picks a sponsor already playing, who vouches for them by real name. Three strikes and a sponsor can vouch no more.
Crusade
November only. Proclaimed against one player for 72 hours; crusaders’ bands beside the same square strike as one, share losses and spoils, and can pay marches into a shared treasury.
Trojan horse
12 to 30 November. Tap another player’s standing tower and choose Send a Trojan horse here, or use the horse button beside the map. It rolls from your nearest castle at 30 minutes a square and costs 1 grain every 4 hours. It carries goods, which arrive a tenth bigger, or soldiers from the band at that castle, who storm the tower’s square at once (walls don’t count). Every horse must carry a letter, read by the tower’s owner on arrival or by whoever brings it down. Only the sender sees what else is inside. Any other player with a standing tower or trebuchet within 4 squares may strike it down for free; the wreck lies on the map for 3 days.
The map
One board for everyone, 120 squares across, wrapping at every edge.
