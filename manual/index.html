The numbers, for reference

Marches earned
5 × √km, where 1,350 steps ≈ 1 km
Claiming
Once a day, for that day’s steps from the Health app, up to 30,000; unclaimed days are lost; every claim is posted to the claim log
Disputes
Anyone else can dispute a claim for 12 hours; the other players then vote for up to 24 hours; nothing is frozen meanwhile
Voiding
Needs a majority of the other players. A voided claim’s marches come off the claimant’s next claim
Empty cell
1 march
Occupied cell
2 marches, plus a soldier per wall course and per gun covering it
Wall
30 stone a course, three courses
Tower
40 stone + 30 timber; garrison 1, 2, then 4 by level
Soldier
25 iron + 40 grain to muster, and he eats every tick thereafter
Harvest
Every 4 hours — 6 ticks a day, banking up to 3 days while you're away
Yields per tick
Forest 2 timber · Quarry 2 stone · Vein 1 iron · Field 3 grain
